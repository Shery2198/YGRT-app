Design a complete responsive website for a modern yogurt brand called **YGRT.**

## Brand

Brand name: **YGRT.**

Primary tagline:
**“less processing, more tasting”**

Brand positioning:
YGRT is a homemade-style yogurt brand focused on delivering rich, creamy, high-quality yogurt without unnecessary additives or excessive processing. The brand aims to offer a premium eating experience at an accessible price.

The website should feel:

* Fresh
* Premium but affordable
* Natural
* Minimalist
* Modern
* Healthy
* Youthful
* Approachable
* Clean and trustworthy

Do NOT make the website look like a generic corporate health-food website. It should feel like a contemporary food/lifestyle brand that could appeal to young adults, fitness enthusiasts, families, and health-conscious consumers.

---

# VISUAL DIRECTION

Use the existing YGRT presentation as the primary visual reference.

### Colour palette

Base the visual system around:

* Warm off-white / cream background
* Soft beige / taupe
* Warm sand
* Black / near-black typography
* Very subtle light grey decorative elements
* Small amounts of natural green where appropriate for health/probiotic messaging

Avoid:

* Bright neon colours
* Excessive gradients
* Generic blue healthcare colours
* Overly colourful layouts
* Excessive shadows

The overall website should have a warm, editorial food-brand aesthetic.

### Typography

Use a bold, modern sans-serif for large headings.

Headings should be:

* Large
* Heavy
* High contrast
* Slightly condensed if an appropriate font is available

Body text should be clean, highly readable, and relatively compact.

Use strong typographic hierarchy.

Example heading treatment:

**Why choose
our yogurt?**

Use lowercase typography selectively to reinforce the contemporary branding.

# RESPONSIVE DESIGN

Design desktop first at approximately **1440px width**.

Also create responsive layouts for:

* Tablet
* Mobile

On mobile:

* Convert navigation into a hamburger menu
* Stack hero content vertically
* Stack product cards
* Convert multi-column sections into vertical sections
* Maintain large typography but scale it appropriately
* Keep CTAs easy to tap
* Preserve the warm editorial aesthetic

# IMPORTANT DESIGN PRINCIPLE

The website should feel like a **real yogurt brand website**, not a presentation converted directly into webpages.

Take the information and visual identity from the supplied YGRT presentation, but transform it into a polished commercial website.

The final design should communicate:

**Natural → Creamy → Healthy → Affordable → Convenient**

The most important visual priorities are:

1. Beautiful yogurt photography
2. Strong typography
3. Warm minimalist colour palette
4. Generous whitespace
5. Clear product/order experience
6. Strong brand identity
7. Modern editorial layout
8. Mobile responsiveness

Do not overcrowd the website with text. Convert the presentation's information into short, highly readable website sections while preserving the original meaning and claims.
